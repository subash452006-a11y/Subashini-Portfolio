How I built this

Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with help from AI tools. I customised the design, wrote the content and deployed it myself. The kolam dots in the hero are drawn on a small canvas. They drift on their own, and you can move your cursor or finger over them to light them up. The kolam motifs between sections are small SVGs. With reduced motion turned on, the dots stay still.
